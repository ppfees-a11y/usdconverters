What the numbers mean

Rates are published reference rates, not a guaranteed retail or bank rate. Rates are not continuously live. Banks, card issuers, and transfer companies may apply spreads, fees, and timing differences. Always confirm a provider's final quote.

For budgeting, multiply the source amount by the published rate. For a real transaction, compare the amount the recipient will receive after all fees.
