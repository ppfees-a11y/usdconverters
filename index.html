What makes an exchange rate useful?

A rate is only meaningful with a currency pair, direction, timestamp, and provider. A mid-market rate shown on a financial website is often different from the rate a bank or payment app actually offers you. A small difference in the quoted rate can matter significantly on large transfers.

Use the calculator to verify arithmetic for a quoted rate, then compare the final amount the provider says the recipient will receive. A transparent quote includes all fees and any conversion spread.
