Frequently asked questions

Are these live exchange rates?

No. They are published reference rates and can differ from continuously changing trading or retail rates.

Why does my bank show a different result?

Banks and payment providers can apply their own exchange rates, spreads, fees, and settlement times.

What happens on weekends?

The historical tool may return the most recent prior published observation. Always check the observation date.

Can I convert USD to multiple currencies?

Yes. Select the target currency and convert; the batch tool converts multiple amounts for one selected pair.

Does the batch tool upload my amounts?

Amounts are processed in your browser. The selected currency pair is sent to the reference-rate API to obtain the rate.

Can I use these numbers for tax filings?

Use the exchange-rate source and method required by your tax authority or accountant. This tool does not provide individualized tax advice.

Why do conversions sometimes fail?

The reference-rate service may be unavailable or a currency pair may not be supported. The calculator displays an error instead of a guessed value.
