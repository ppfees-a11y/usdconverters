Frequently asked questions

Are the exchange rates live?

No. This site does not fetch live rates. Enter a rate from a source you trust, ideally noting its timestamp.

Why do I need to enter the rate?

It prevents a stale or made-up value from appearing to be an executable market quote and lets you calculate against a provider’s actual offer.

How do I convert euros into dollars?

Enter the EUR-per-USD rate, select foreign currency → USD, and divide your EUR amount by that rate.

Does the result include all bank charges?

Only the fixed and percentage fees you enter. A bank may use its own exchange rate, spread, minimum fee, or intermediary charges.

What is a currency spread?

It is the difference between the buy and sell rates or between a provider’s quote and a reference rate. It can increase the effective conversion cost.

Can I use the calculator for travel?

Yes, for estimating conversions based on a rate you enter. For a card purchase, check the issuer’s foreign transaction fees and network conversion rules.

Why do two providers show different amounts?

They may use different exchange rates, fee structures, transaction times, or settlement routes.

Does this website send money?

No. It is an educational calculator only; it cannot initiate transfers or guarantee a payout.

What if the currency has no decimal places?

The calculator uses standard readable rounding where available, but the actual payment provider determines settlement precision.

How do I compare two offers?

Use the comparison tool on the home page for a rate-only comparison, then account for each provider’s total fees and delivered amount.
