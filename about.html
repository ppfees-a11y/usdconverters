About USDConverters

USDConverters is a free, U.S.-focused utility for exchange-rate reference conversions, historical comparisons, provider fee planning, and spreadsheet-ready batch calculations.

Created by James Gore. The site is independently operated and is not affiliated with Frankfurter, any bank, or a money transfer provider.

We prioritize visible source dates, transparent assumptions, and calculators that explain their limitations.
