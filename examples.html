Currency conversion examples

Example 1: simple conversion

At an illustrative rate of 0.90 EUR per USD, $1,000 × 0.90 = €900 before fees. This is a mathematical example, not a current exchange-rate quote.

Example 2: rate markup

Suppose the reference conversion is €900, a $5 fee is deducted from $1,000, and the exchange rate is reduced by 2%. Estimated delivery: ($1,000 − $5) × 0.90 × 0.98 = €877.59.

Example 3: reverse currency pair

If one USD buys 0.80 units of another currency, one unit of that currency buys 1 ÷ 0.80 = 1.25 USD. Rounding can make displayed inverse rates differ slightly.

Example 4: bulk invoice review

Converting 25, 100, and 250 source units at 1.20 target units per source unit yields 30, 120, and 300 target units, respectively. Export the batch for your records.
