Worked currency conversion examples

All rates below are hypothetical teaching examples, not live exchange-rate quotes.

Example 1 — USD to EUR

Suppose you have $250 and a quoted rate of 0.90 EUR per USD. Multiply: 250 × 0.90 = €225. This assumes no fees.

Example 2 — EUR to USD

Suppose you have €180 and a quoted rate of 0.90 EUR per USD. Divide: 180 ÷ 0.90 = $200. This assumes no fees.

Example 3 — Fixed and percentage fees

Suppose you send $1,000, the provider charges $5 fixed plus 1% of the original source amount, and the rate is 0.90 EUR per USD. The percentage fee is $10; net convertible amount = $1,000 − $5 − $10 = $985. The illustrative recipient amount is €886.50. Actual provider fee rules can differ.

Example 4 — Rate comparison

Provider A quotes 0.900 EUR/USD and Provider B quotes 0.895 EUR/USD for a $2,000 conversion. A yields €1,800; B yields €1,790. The difference is €10 before any fees. A better headline rate does not always mean a better final delivered amount.

Example 5 — Rate inversion

If 1 USD = 150 JPY in a hypothetical quote, then 1 JPY = 1/150 USD, approximately $0.006667. Dividing a JPY amount by 150 gives its USD equivalent at that rate.
