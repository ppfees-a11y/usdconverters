Calculation methodology and exchange-rate data

Reference-rate source

Our calculator requests the Frankfurter public API. The provider aggregates published reference rates from institutional sources. The site uses the v1 latest and dated endpoints; these are not tick-by-tick trading quotes.

Formulas

Conversion: converted amount = source amount × reference rate.

Estimated provider amount: (source amount − fixed fee) × reference rate × (1 − markup percentage ÷ 100). Negative fee-adjusted amounts are treated as zero.

Reverse rate: if the base/quote rate is nonzero, the reciprocal gives the quote/base rate.

Precision and rounding

Intermediate calculations use JavaScript numeric arithmetic and the published rate. Results are displayed with currency-appropriate formatting; CSV batch results are rounded to two decimals. For accounting, follow your organization's required rounding and source policies.

Data freshness and outages

The interface displays the provider's observation date. If the service cannot be reached, the site shows an error rather than inventing a rate. No fabricated fallback rates are provided.

Research benchmark

Currency tools at Xe and Wise offer rate charts and historical context. This site focuses on free reference conversion, historical dates, a transparent fee model, and CSV batch exports without account creation.
