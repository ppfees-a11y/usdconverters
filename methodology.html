Calculation methodology

Transparent formulas and assumptions so you can check every result.

Currency conversion formulas

Let R be the number of foreign-currency units per one US dollar. For a fee-free conversion: foreign amount = USD amount × R; USD amount = foreign amount ÷ R. R must be positive.

Fee model

For the optional fee estimate, the percentage fee is calculated from the original source amount: percentage fee = source amount × fee% / 100. Then net source amount = source amount − fixed fee − percentage fee. The net source amount is converted at the entered rate. If fees exceed the source amount, the calculator reports an error instead of a negative payout.

The fixed fee is denominated in the source currency. This is a simplified comparison model, not a promise of how any particular provider calculates fees. The tool does not independently estimate a hidden exchange-rate spread.

Quote comparison and precision

For two rates A and B, each quoted as foreign units per USD, the difference in foreign units is USD amount × (A − B). Calculations use JavaScript numeric precision. Displayed results are rounded for readability; a provider may apply currency-specific minor-unit rules and rounding at intermediate stages.

Data sources and freshness

No external rates are fetched. You must supply your own quoted rate. Currency names and symbols are display conveniences, not evidence of a current rate. No exchange-rate provider is endorsed, and no transaction is executed on this site.

Limitations

Quoted prices can vary with timing, order size, settlement method, intermediary institutions, and customer eligibility. Tax and reporting requirements may apply to some transactions. Confirm final terms with your provider.
